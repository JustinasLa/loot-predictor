What is a save file?

Your Eatventure save contains the numbers this tool needs to predict your boxes. If you already have a JSON copy, choose it with “Load save file”. If you don’t, try the demo to explore how it works.

We read your save in your browser and never upload or change the file. This browser remembers your box progress until you remove your save.

This tool previews your save. Open boxes in Eatventure, then mark them as opened here to keep your predictions in sync.

Made for the Eatventure community. An independent fan tool, not affiliated with Eatventure or its creators.
