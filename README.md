# Loot Lookout

> See what's in your next Eatventure box.

Loot Lookout is a loot predictor for Eatventure. Load your save and it shows, in order, the exact items each of your boxes, eggs and adventure chests will drop, with the real game art, names and rarity colours.

Everything runs in the browser. Your save is read on your own device, never uploaded, and never changed. Try it at [justinasla.github.io/loot-predictor](https://justinasla.github.io/loot-predictor/), or tap **Try demo** to explore without a save.

![Next drops for the Big Box](docs/screenshots/next-desktop.png)

## Features

- **Next drops** — every upcoming opening, in order, with the real item art on rarity-coloured tiles.
- **One-tap tracking** — tap **I opened it!** after opening a box in the game to stay in step, with **Undo** for mistakes.
- **Find an item** — tap the pictures of the items you want and see how many openings away they are.
- **XP planner** — for Zeus and Pirate adventures, picks the level for each opening that earns the most XP, vault keys included.
- **Every box** — Big, Small, Pet and Club boxes, eggs, event boxes and adventure chests.
- **Private by design** — the tracker is stored only in your browser, and **Remove from this device** clears it.
- **Built for phones** — the layout follows the phone you play on, and works just as well on desktop.
